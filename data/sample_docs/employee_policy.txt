Acme Corp Employee Leave Policy
================================

Annual Leave
------------
The company provides 20 days of annual leave per calendar year for full-time employees.
Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next year.

Sick Leave
----------
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave each year.
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Notice Period
-------------
Employees must provide at least 14 days of advance notice when requesting annual leave,
except in emergency situations approved by a manager.

Holiday Closures
----------------
The company observes national public holidays. These days do not count against annual leave.
